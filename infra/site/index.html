Why NexCRM exists

CRMs tend to drift: definitions blur, fields rot, and pipeline turns into a story. NexCRM keeps the system honest.

Replace "admin work" with guardrails

FAQ

Straight answers. If you want something specific, email hello@nexcrm.io.

Is NexCRM "another Salesforce"? ▾

No. NexCRM is intentionally narrower: it's focused on pipeline hygiene, activity integrity, and forecasting signal. It's built for operators who want a system that stays clean without constant clean-up projects.

What does "activity you can trust" actually mean? ▾

It means touches are normalized and attributed consistently across sources, and stage movement can be tied to evidence. The goal is to reduce fake busywork and make coaching easier.

Can we customize it for our process? ▾

Yes, through rules, definitions, and review queues. The customization is designed to stay understandable, not become a brittle maze of fields.

Do you support SSO? ▾

Planned for Enterprise. If you need SAML/SCIM early, email us and we'll align roadmap to your requirements.
